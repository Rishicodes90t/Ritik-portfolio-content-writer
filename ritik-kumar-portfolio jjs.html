The Wordsmith

Words, seen
through a design lens

"I think about a sentence the way I think about a screen — what earns attention, what builds trust, and what makes someone want to keep going."

I'm Ritik — a content writer and copywriter with a background that doesn't follow the usual path into writing. I started in audio editing, moved into UI/UX design, and along the way kept coming back to the same thing: words, and how to make them land.

Since 2023 I've worked as an audio editor and content writer at Sukudio Analytica, where I translate, adapt, and write dubbing scripts for micro-drama platforms like Kuku TV and DashReels, and edit and write content for creator YouTube channels — including Hindi content for Nikhil Kamath's podcast, and localized content for family channels like Teen Times, WooHoo, and Slick Slime Sam. Alongside that, I freelance as a UI/UX designer, and earlier wrote scripts and edited videos as an independent content creator for social platforms. That mix — translation, sound, screens, and story — is exactly what I bring to AI-to-human content: I know what makes a sentence sound stiff, a script sound unnatural, or an interface sound unhelpful, and how to fix it.

I lean heavily on AI tools in my process — for research, ideation, and first drafts — and then do the real work: rewriting until it sounds like an actual person, not a model. That's the part I care about most.

Outside of client work, I'm a BA graduate (School of Open Learning), and I write and speak in Hindi, English, and Bengali.
